Manhattan and Q-Q plots, straight from your results file

Drop in output from PLINK, REGENIE, SAIGE, BOLT-LMM, METAL or anything with a chromosome, position and p-value column. Millions of rows are fine. Everything runs locally in this tab.

  • Checks your data first. Missing values, p-values out of range, p = 0, odd chromosome names and more are reported before anything is plotted.
  • Interactive. Hover for details, drag to zoom into a region, click points to label them.
  • Publication ready. Export high-resolution PNG or editable SVG, and copy the equivalent qqman R code.
Drag to zoom · double-click to reset · click a point to label it
